Raikor

Age: 34

Description: A battle-hardened warrior forged in the crucible of war. His cybernetic limbs and high-intensity laser cannon make him a force of destruction.

Story: Once a soldier in the Galactic Enforcers, Raikor was betrayed and left for dead. Rebuilt with cybernetic enhancements, he now seeks vengeance against those who wronged him.

Abilities:

  • Laser Cannon Barrage: Unleashes a devastating volley of energy blasts.
  • Cybernetic Strength: Enhanced limbs allow superhuman strength and agility.
  • Thermal Vision: Detects enemies through walls and in darkness.

Nexla

Age: 28

Description: A master of stealth, using cloaking armor and plasma daggers to strike from the shadows.

Story: Raised in the underbelly of a dystopian megacity, Nexla honed her skills as an assassin. She now operates as a freelance operative, taking contracts to destabilize corrupt regimes.

Abilities:

  • Cloaking Field: Renders her invisible for short periods.
  • Plasma Daggers: Dual-wielded blades that cut through armor.
  • Shadow Step: Teleports short distances to evade or flank enemies.
